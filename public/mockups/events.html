    Before the day

    How much notice do you need for an event?

    48 to 72 hours when freezers or a serving counter are involved; 24 hours for ice cream alone. Name-sleeve packs need 5 to 7 working days.

    What if the venue has no power?

    We pack the order in insulated boxes with food-grade dry ice, which holds it frozen for 6 to 8 hours. Tick “Outdoor venue” in the calculator and it is added for you.

    Can the guest count change after I confirm?

    Yes, up to 24 hours before delivery at no charge. On the day, our van can usually bring top-ups within the core city.

    How do I pay?

    No payment on the website. We send a proforma invoice; events take 50% in advance by UPI or bank transfer, the balance on delivery.
