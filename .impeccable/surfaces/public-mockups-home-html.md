# Surface brief: Shaadi Card mockups (Home, Events, Catalogue, Product)

Scope: the replacement visual world, first as static mockups under `public/mockups/`, then adopted across the live site on 2026-10-12 at the owner's request ("build this design"). Dark by default, with a light mode toggle.
Mode: Persuade (Home, Events); Catalogue and Product lean Operate inside the same world.
Audience/job: caterers, wedding planners and corporate buyers sizing an event, plus restaurants and shops restocking. Action: send a quote request, or WhatsApp.
Proof: none real. Every stat, testimonial and client is labelled illustrative.
Constraints: floating Get Bulk Quote and WhatsApp on every page; no online payment; avoid kiddy/candy, corporate/cold, neon, and anything Airbnb.
Memorable moment: the invitation card's own fields (Event · Date · Venue · Guests) are the quote form, and the order is answered on a matching menu card.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The quote is a wedding invitation. Event, date, venue and guests are set in foil on indigo board, and the order comes back like the matching menu card. It refuses the category default of a split hero, a product photo and a row of equal category cards.

OWN-WORLD: Indigo board ground, gold-foil line work in double-ruled frames and diamond-centred rules, vermilion for the single action, ivory card stock as the light mode. Yatra One display lettering, Hind for text and tabular figures. Square letterpress corners, no shadows, no rounded pills.

STORY: The visitor sees their own event written as an invitation, changes the guest count, watches cartons and cost update, believes the supplier handles the whole celebration, and sends the card as a quote request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Hairline-ruled centred nav. Below it, one centred invitation card about 880px wide on indigo: a large two-line display headline, one invitation sentence, then a foil-ruled row of four editable fields (Event, Date, Venue, Guests), a live estimate line, and the vermilion "Request the quote" as the primary action inside the card.

FORM: Shaadi Card (letterpress Indian wedding invitation), position 1 on my ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK); seed key f625f842.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
